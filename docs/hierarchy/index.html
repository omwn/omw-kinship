OMW kinship hierarchy

Each concept is shown as its kin-type features: xElBr is a man's elder brother. A concept sits below every concept whose positions contain its own, so many concepts appear under more than one hypernym. See FEATURES.md for the algebra.

Words marked ◇ were taken automatically from existing wordnets, through each concept's link to the Interlingual Index, and have not been checked. A wordnet may list a narrower word for a broader concept (Arabic جدّ “grandfather” for grandparent) or a rare or literary one. Where they contradict a gap KinDiv records, the gap is not shown.
